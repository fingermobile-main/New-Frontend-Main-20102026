## 1. Quad-Separation Architecture

The FingerMobile Protocol enforces strict operational, infrastructural, and legal separation across four e-commerce portals:

1. **Marketplace (/marketplace):** The official platform flagship store for FingerMobile merchandise, verified partner electronics, and hardware.
2. **Storefront (/storefront):** Sovereign merchant portal for subscription members, artisans, and content creators with self-service inventory management.
3. **OwnIt (/ownit):** High-end red-carpet luxury boutique with Cryptographic Certificates of Authenticity (CoA) and VIP courier delivery.
4. **Flamingo (/flamingo):** Discrete intimacy and luxury runway fashion boutique protected by Dual-Lock statutory 19+ age gate.

## 2. Escrow & Settlement Rails

### 2.1 Verified Escrow Protection
- Transactions processed through Marketplace and Storefront are secured by the FingerMobile Escrow Engine.
- Merchant disbursements occur upon verified shipping delivery confirmation or completed buyer inspection windows.

### 2.2 Currency & FIC Discounts
- Pricing may be displayed in multi-currency FIAT alongside FingerMobile Infinite Coins (FIC).
- Members utilizing FIC tokens receive instant cryptographic settlement discounts.

## 3. Merchant Obligations & Returns

- **Authenticity Warranty:** Merchants warrant that all physical or digital goods listed are authentic and non-infringing.
- **Delivery Commitments:** Standard shipping timelines must be honored with tracking integration.
- **Refund Policy:** Escrow disputes may be escalated to the platform dispute resolution council within 14 days of order receipt.
