# AI Usage Policy for FingerMobile Protocol

**Effective Date:** [DATE]

This AI Usage Policy ("Policy") governs the use of Artificial Intelligence (AI) features and services provided by FingerMobile ("we", "us", or "our") on the FingerMobile Protocol platform ("Platform") and its associated services ("Services"). This Policy supplements our [Terms of Service](./02_Terms_of_Service.md) and [Privacy Policy](./01_Privacy_Policy.md).

By using any AI features on our Platform, you agree to be bound by this Policy.

## 1. Ethical Use

You must use our AI features responsibly and ethically. You agree not to:
*   Use AI to generate illegal, harmful, defamatory, pornographic, discriminatory, or otherwise objectionable content.
*   Use AI to deceive, manipulate, or scam others.
*   Use AI to violate the intellectual property rights of others.
*   Use AI to create deepfakes or synthetic media intended to mislead without appropriate labeling.
*   Attempt to reverse-engineer, hack, or exploit vulnerabilities in our AI systems.

## 2. Content Generation

*   **Responsibility:** You are responsible for the input you provide to AI features and the output generated, especially if you choose to share it.
*   **Provenance:** Be transparent when sharing AI-generated content if it might be mistaken for human-created content.
*   **Privacy:** Do not input sensitive personal information or confidential data into AI features unless explicitly required and secured.

## 3. Data Usage for AI Improvement

*   **Training:** FingerMobile may use anonymized and aggregated user interactions with AI features to improve our AI models, subject to our [Privacy Policy](./01_Privacy_Policy.md). This process ensures individual user data is not directly linked to the resulting improvements.
*   **Opt-Out:** Where technically feasible, we may offer options to opt out of having your specific inputs used for model training.

## 4. Transparency and Limitations

*   **Awareness:** AI systems can sometimes produce inaccurate, biased, or unexpected results. Be aware of these limitations.
*   **Labeling:** Where possible, AI-generated content produced by our systems will be labeled as such.

## 5. Prohibited Uses (Specific to AI)

Beyond the general acceptable use rules, AI features must not be used for:
*   Automated mass creation of accounts or content (bots).
*   Circumventing content moderation.
*   Generating malware or other harmful code.
*   Large-scale harvesting or scraping of data specifically for training competing AI models without permission.

## 6. Compliance

Your use of AI features must comply with all applicable laws and regulations, including those concerning AI ethics and data protection.

## 7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this AI Usage Policy. We will notify you of material changes. Your continued use after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
