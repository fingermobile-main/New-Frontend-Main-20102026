# FingerMobile Community Guidelines

**Effective Date:** [DATE]

These Community Guidelines ("Guidelines") outline the standards for behavior and content on the FingerMobile Protocol platform ("Platform"). They complement our [Terms of Service](./02_Terms_of_Service.md) and [Privacy Policy](./01_Privacy_Policy.md).

By using the Platform, you agree to abide by these Guidelines. Violations may result in content removal, account restrictions, or termination.

## 1. Respect Others

Treat everyone with respect. Harassment, threats, stalking, intimidation, and impersonation are prohibited.

## 2. Hate Speech and Discrimination

Content that promotes violence, hatred, or discrimination against individuals or groups based on race, ethnicity, national origin, religion, disability, gender, sexual orientation, age, or other protected characteristics is not allowed.

## 3. Violence and Harm

Do not threaten, promote, or glorify violence. This includes graphic depictions of violence, instructions for making weapons, and encouragement of self-harm.

## 4. Misinformation and Spam

Share accurate information. Do not spread false or misleading information that can cause harm. Avoid posting repetitive, unsolicited, or irrelevant content (spam).

## 5. Privacy and Safety

Respect others' privacy. Do not share private information without consent. Do not engage in doxxing. Report safety concerns promptly.

## 6. Intellectual Property

Respect intellectual property rights. Do not share copyrighted content you do not own without permission. Respect trademarks.

## 7. Illegal Activities

Do not use the Platform for illegal activities or to promote illegal goods or services.

## 8. Enforcement

We may use automated systems and human reviewers to enforce these Guidelines. Penalties range from content removal to account suspension or termination, depending on the severity and frequency of violations.

## 9. Appeals

If you believe a moderation decision was made in error, you can appeal the decision through our appeals process.

## 10. Updates

These Guidelines may be updated periodically. Users will be notified of significant changes.
