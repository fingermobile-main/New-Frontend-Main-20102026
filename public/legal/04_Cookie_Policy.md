## 1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device (computer, mobile, tablet) when you visit a website. Similar technologies include web beacons, pixels, and mobile identifiers. They help recognize your device and remember certain information.

## 2. How We Use Cookies

We use cookies for various purposes to operate and enhance our Services:

*   **Strictly Necessary Cookies:** Essential for basic platform functions like logging in, navigating pages, and managing sessions. These cookies are usually enabled by default.
*   **Performance and Analytics Cookies:** Collect information about how visitors use our Services (e.g., popular pages, errors encountered). This helps us improve performance and understand user behavior. These may be provided by third-party analytics services like Google Analytics.
*   **Functionality Cookies:** Remember choices you make (e.g., language, region, theme) to provide enhanced, personalized features.
*   **Targeting/Advertising Cookies:** Used to deliver relevant advertisements to you and measure their effectiveness. These may also limit the number of times you see an ad. These may be managed by third-party advertising partners.

## 3. Managing Cookies

Most web browsers allow you to control cookies through their settings. You can typically accept or reject all cookies or be notified when a cookie is set. However, disabling certain cookies may impact your experience on our Services.

For mobile applications, you can manage tracking preferences through your device settings.
