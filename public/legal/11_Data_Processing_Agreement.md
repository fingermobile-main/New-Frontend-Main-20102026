# FingerMobile Protocol Data Processing Agreement (DPA)

**Version:** 2.0.0
**Effective Date:** October 9, 2026
**Governing Entity:** GraceLink Business Technologies Limited
**Applicable Frameworks:** Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA 2023), GDPR (Regulation (EU) 2016/679) & CCPA

---

## 1. Purpose & Scope

This Data Processing Agreement ("DPA") governs the collection, processing, encryption, and transfer of personal data by GraceLink Business Technologies Limited on behalf of protocol members, enterprise merchants, and third-party developers.

---

## 2. Core Processing Tenets

In accordance with NDPA 2023 and GDPR Article 28:

1. **Lawfulness & Transparency:** Data is processed exclusively on lawful grounds (consent, contractual necessity, or statutory compliance).
2. **Purpose Limitation:** Personal data collected for authentication or settlement is never diverted for unauthorized advertising profiling.
3. **Data Minimization:** Only data strictly essential for protocol operations is retained.
4. **Storage Limitation & Erasure:** Inactive accounts are scheduled for permanent purge within statutory timelines.
5. **Confidentiality & Security:** AES-256 at rest, TLS 1.3 in transit, and multi-factor TOTP authentication.
6. **Accountability:** Full cryptographic audit logging within the Cognitive-Legal Vault.

---

## 3. Data Subject Rights (DSR)

Users maintain enforceable rights to:
- Access, download, and port their data via `/data-subject-request` (processed within 30 days).
- Request permanent cascading erasure under NDPA Section 34 via `/account-deletion`.
- Lodge inquiries directly with our designated Data Protection Officer (DPO) at `/dpo`.
