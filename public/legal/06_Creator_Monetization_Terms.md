# FingerMobile Protocol Creator Monetization Terms

**Version:** 2.0.0
**Effective Date:** October 9, 2026
**Governing Entity:** GraceLink Business Technologies Limited
**Governing Law:** Republic of Singapore

---

## 1. Scope & Eligibility

These Creator Monetization Terms ("Creator Terms") govern your participation in the FingerMobile Creator Program, monetization tools, tipping engines, paid episode unlocks, Paylinks, and affiliate partnerships.

### 1.1 Qualification & KYC
To activate monetization features, creators must:
- Be at least 18 years of age (22 years for independent commercial entity contracting).
- Complete identity verification (KYC/KYB) via the FingerMobile Verification Console.
- Maintain account standing with zero unresolved Sentinel community strikes.

---

## 2. Monetization Mechanisms

### 2.1 Infinite Coins (FIC) & Tips
- Members may reward creators with FingerMobile Infinite Coins (FIC) and digital gifts (🎁).
- Tips and paid episode unlocks are settled into the creator's Pending Earnings ledger.

### 2.2 Subscriptions & Paylinks
- Creators may establish custom subscription tiers for exclusive feeds, channels, or groups.
- Paylinks (🔗) enable direct transactional billing for bespoke digital assets, consultations, or premium media.

---

## 3. Revenue Settlement & Payout Schedule

### 3.1 Payout Threshold & Currency
- Payouts are denominated in FIC tokens with 1-click settlement to verified bank accounts or authorized digital wallets.
- Standard platform processing fee is deducted transparently prior to disbursement.

### 3.2 Dispute Window
- Creators must review monthly earnings statements and submit any discrepancy notices within thirty (30) calendar days of generation.

### 3.3 Forfeiture & Violations
- Engagement in artificial engagement manipulation, fake streaming pools, fraudulent coupon schemes, or copyright infringement results in immediate forfeiture of pending revenue and suspension of creator entitlements.
