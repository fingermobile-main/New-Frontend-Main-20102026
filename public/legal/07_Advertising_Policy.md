# FingerMobile Protocol Advertising Policy

**Version:** 2.0.0
**Effective Date:** October 9, 2026
**Governing Entity:** GraceLink Business Technologies Limited
**Governing Law:** Republic of Singapore

---

## 1. Overview & Scope

This Advertising Policy governs all promotional campaigns, sponsored placements, Canary Ads, Canary Sponsor hero slots, and Publicitee campaigns published across the FingerMobile Protocol.

---

## 2. Content Standards for Advertisers

All advertising creatives, sponsored cards, and media assets must adhere to the following standards:

### 2.1 Prohibited Categories
- Deceptive financial schemes, unauthorized multi-level marketing, and unregistered securities.
- Counterfeit luxury goods, unlicensed pharmaceuticals, or prohibited weapons.
- Politically or religiously divisive campaigns (strictly prohibited under platform neutrality policy).
- Malware, adware, spyware, or predatory redirects.

### 2.2 Restricted Categories (Special Scaffolding Required)
- Adult wellness & intimacy products: Restricted exclusively to the age-gated Flamingo boutique (/flamingo) with Dual-Lock 19+ verification.
- Micro-lending promotions: Restricted exclusively to verified LoanSqr partner conduits.

---

## 3. Canary Ads & Attribution Standards

- **Transparency:** All commercial placements must display clear "Sponsored" or "Canary Sponsor" badges.
- **Attribution & Privacy:** User impression and click analytics are collected pseudonymously in full compliance with NDPA 2023 and GDPR guidelines.
- **Review Pipeline:** Campaigns pass through automated Sentinel quality filters prior to public display.
