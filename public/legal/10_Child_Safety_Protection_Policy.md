## 1. Absolute Zero-Tolerance Mandate

GraceLink Business Technologies Limited enforces an uncompromising zero-tolerance stance against Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM) and Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE) across all communication channels, media feeds, private messages, and cloud repositories of the FingerMobile Protocol.

## 2. Automated Perceptual Hashing & Sentinel Sentinel
