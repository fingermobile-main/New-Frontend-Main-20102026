# SuperVi: Orbs of Glory - Rise of the Six
## Game Design Document

### 1. Executive Summary

SuperVi: Orbs of Glory - Rise of the Six is an Afro-futuristic Urban Superhero Adventure game that combines procedural generation, level design, and an engaging reward system. The game features six unique superheroes from different African ethnic backgrounds, each with special abilities tied to an Orb of Glory with Egyptian symbols.

### 2. Game Concept

**Core Gameplay**: Endless runner with procedurally generated levels set in an urban jungle environment that blends cityscapes with wild jungle elements.

**Unique Selling Point**: Afro-futuristic theme with authentic African cultural representation, featuring six diverse superheroes with unique abilities and backstories.

### 3. Target Audience

- Primary: Ages 10-35 interested in action-adventure games
- Secondary: Fans of Afro-futuristic themes and diverse representation
- Tertiary: Mobile gamers looking for engaging, story-driven experiences

### 4. Core Mechanics

#### 4.1 Procedural Level Generation
- **Tile-Based Spawning**: Unity spawns pre-modeled "tiles" (sections of bush/forest tracks with obstacles)
- **Time Intervals**: 2-3 seconds, then 5-8 seconds, then 6-8 seconds, then 3-5 seconds, then 11-19 seconds (variable timing to keep gameplay unpredictable)
- **Obstacle Types**: Urban debris, jungle vines, wild animals, ancient structures, moving platforms

#### 4.2 Character Abilities
Each character has unique abilities tied to their superhero identity:
- **Zara the Zephr**: Fire manipulation and levitation
- **Tenko-Gen**: Stretching and high-altitude navigation
- **King the Guardian**: Strength and barrier destruction
- **Phantom Shadow**: Phasing and invisibility
- **Bola the Brave**: Lightning speed and protective carrying
- **Neoma the Healer**: Protective barriers and healing

#### 4.3 Orb of Glory System
- Each character wields a unique Orb of Glory with Egyptian symbols
- Orbs provide special abilities and can be upgraded
- Collecting Orb Fragments allows for orb enhancement
- Orbs have unique visual effects and particle systems

### 5. Visual Design

#### 5.1 Art Style
- Afro-futuristic aesthetic blending traditional African elements with sci-fi technology
- High-contrast, saturated colors with glowing neon accents
- Jungle-urban hybrid environments with wild animals integrated into cityscapes
- Character designs featuring authentic cultural elements (hijabs, traditional patterns, etc.)

#### 5.2 Environments
- **Primary Setting**: Urban jungle in African cities (Abuja, Lagos, Kano)
- **Secondary Locations**: Madrid, France, Ethiopia with similar urban-jungle fusion
- **Visual Elements**: Lush green vines growing through modern buildings, forest tracks replacing streets, wild animals roaming urban areas
- **Atmospheric Effects**: Floating Egyptian symbols as mystical energy, dynamic lighting, particle systems

### 6. Audio Design

#### 6.1 Soundtrack
- Afrobeat and electronic fusion
- Traditional African instruments mixed with modern synths
- Dynamic music that changes with gameplay intensity
- Character-specific themes

#### 6.2 Sound Effects
- Environmental sounds (jungle, city, animals)
- Character ability feedback
- Orb interaction sounds
- UI and menu sounds

### 7. Progression System

#### 7.1 Currency
- **Coins**: Earned during runs, used for minor upgrades and cosmetics
- **Keys**: Premium currency for character unlocks and major upgrades
- **Orb Fragments**: Rare currency for orb enhancement and special abilities

#### 7.2 Character Progression
- Level up characters to unlock new abilities
- Collect character-specific items to enhance performance
- Unlock new visual customization options

#### 7.3 Orb Enhancement
- Upgrade orb abilities and visual effects
- Unlock new orb powers
- Enhance character-specific orb synergies

### 8. Monetization

#### 8.1 Free-to-Play Model
- Base game is free with six characters available
- In-app purchases for premium currency (keys)
- Cosmetic items and character customization options

#### 8.2 Premium Content
- Additional characters and orbs
- Premium season passes
- Cosmetic bundles and special events

### 9. Technical Specifications

#### 9.1 Platform
- Primary: Mobile (iOS and Android)
- Secondary: PC and console ports planned
- Engine: Unity 3D

#### 9.2 Performance Requirements
- Optimized for mid-range mobile devices
- 60 FPS target on supported devices
- Efficient asset loading and memory management

### 10. Story and Narrative

#### 10.1 World Background
The world of SuperVi exists in a reality where ancient Egyptian powers have merged with modern African urban environments. The Orbs of Glory were scattered across various African cities, awakening six individuals with unique abilities to protect their communities.

#### 10.2 Character Stories
Each character has a personal story arc connected to their cultural background and their relationship with their Orb of Glory. Players discover these stories through gameplay and special narrative events.

### 11. Multiplayer Features

#### 11.1 Cooperative Play
- Team up with friends to complete special missions
- Character synergy mechanics when playing together
- Shared Orb energy for enhanced abilities

#### 11.2 Competitive Elements
- Global leaderboards for high scores
- Seasonal competitions with unique rewards
- Character-specific challenges

### 12. Accessibility Features

- Multiple control options (touch, gesture, button-based)
- Visual and audio alternatives for different needs
- Adjustable difficulty settings
- Colorblind-friendly UI elements

### 13. Post-Launch Content

#### 13.1 Regular Updates
- New characters and orbs monthly
- Seasonal events and limited-time challenges
- Environment and obstacle variety expansion

#### 13.2 Community Features
- Member-generated content tools
- Community challenges and events
- Character and level sharing systems

### 14. Marketing and Launch Strategy

#### 14.1 Pre-Launch
- Cultural consultant involvement to ensure respectful representation
- Partnership with African cultural organizations
- Influencer campaigns with diverse content creators

#### 14.2 Launch Campaign
- Emphasis on cultural authenticity and representation
- Gameplay reveals highlighting unique character abilities
- Community engagement through social media

This game design document provides a comprehensive roadmap for developing SuperVi: Orbs of Glory - Rise of the Six, ensuring that the game honors its cultural inspirations while delivering an engaging and innovative gaming experience.
