## FingerMobile Community Standards: Nudity and Sexual Activity

We don’t allow the sharing or sending of nudity or sexual activity. While our policies have become more nuanced over time to accommodate educational, medical, artistic, and protest contexts, we restrict this content to keep our community respectful and safe. Because some users may be sensitive to nudity, and to prevent the spread of non-consensual or underage material, we default to removing sexual imagery unless a clear, permitted exception applies.

---

### Core Principles

* **Freedom of Expression:** We want you to share freely with others, and we only restrict content or users to maintain a safe, respectful environment.
* **Global Consistency:** We apply the same rules worldwide, utilizing our technology and moderation teams across multiple languages to ensure consistent enforcement.
* **Nuance and Context:** We recognize that nudity and sexual content often serve valid purposes. When intent is clear, allowances are made for health, education, art, protest, and crisis documentation.

---

### Prohibited Content

#### Adult Nudity

We do not allow imagery of adult nudity if it depicts:

* **Sex:** Sex in any variation.
* **Genitalia:** Visible genitalia, except in medical, health, or crisis contexts (e.g., birth and after-birth moments, gender confirmation surgery, disease examinations, famine, genocide, war crimes, or crimes against humanity).
* **Anuses and Buttocks:** Visible anuses and/or fully nude close-ups of buttocks, unless photoshopped onto a public figure or shared within permitted medical/crisis contexts.
* **Female Nipples:** Uncovered female nipples, except in the context of breastfeeding, post-mastectomy scarring, medical or health situations, acts of protest, or famine, genocide, war crimes, and crimes against humanity.

#### Adult Sexual Activity (Real-World)

We do not allow imagery of adult sexual activity, including:

* **Explicit Sexual Activity & Stimulation:** Explicit sexual intercourse or oral sex (indicated by a mouth or genitals in contact with another person's genitals or anus when at least one is visible), and direct stimulation or insertion of sex toys into a person's genitals or anus.
* **Implicit Sexual Activity & Stimulation:** Implicit intercourse, oral sex, or toy insertion/placement where contact or anatomy is not directly visible—*unless* posted in medical, health, or sexual wellness contexts, promotional content, or recognized fictional works.
* **Other Sexual Activities:** Erections, the presence of sexual activity by-products, sex toys placed upon or inserted into the mouth, stimulation of visible human nipples, and breast squeezing (defined as a grabbing motion with curved fingers showing distinct shape change, *except* in breastfeeding contexts)—*unless* shared in medical, health, or fictional contexts.

#### Fetish Content

We prohibit imagery depicting fetishes that involve:

* Acts likely to lead to the death of a person or animal
* Dismemberment
* Cannibalism
* Feces, urine, spit, snot, menstruation, or vomit
* Bestiality
* Incest

#### Digital Imagery and Audio

* **Digital Sexual Activity:** Prohibited except when posted for medical awareness, scientific discourse, or sexual health discussion, *and* viewing is restricted to adults aged 18 or older.
* **Audio:** Extended audio of sexual activity is prohibited.

---

### Content Labeling and Age Restrictions

To balance safety with user awareness, certain permitted content requires friction or age-gating:

#### Spoiler Warning Labels Required

Content containing visible adult genitalia, close-ups of buttocks, anuses, or implicit/other sexual activity/stimulation shared in a **medical or health context** (such as birth-giving, gender confirmation surgery, or self-examinations) must include a **Spoiler Warning** label.

#### Age-Gated Content (18+ Restrictions)

Viewing is limited to adults aged 18 or older for:

* Real-world art depicting implicit, explicit, or other sexual activity/stimulation.
* Real-world art depicting bestiality neutrally or in condemnation using unreal people/animals.
* Implicit adult sexual activity in advertisements or recognized fictional works.
* Digital imagery and real-world art of non-explicit, non-fetish adult sexual activity posted in a satirical or humorous context, or where only body shapes and contours are visible.

---

### Permitted Exceptions

* **Artistic & Cultural Works:** Photographs of paintings, sculptures, and other art depicting nude figures are allowed.
* **Crisis & Conflict:** All otherwise restricted nudity and sexual content is permitted in the context of famine, genocide, war crimes, or crimes against humanity.
* **Satire and Humor:** Violating elements are permitted if the content genuinely satirizes, mocks, or criticizes the violating elements or the subject they are attributed to.

---

### Reporting Violations

If you encounter content you believe violates these standards across posts, comments, stories, or messages, you can report it:

1. Tap the report option on the specific piece of content.
2. Select the category that best reflects the problem to ensure it routes correctly.
3. Review your details for accuracy and click **Submit**.

Once a report is submitted, our moderation systems review the content. If it violates our Community Standards, it will be removed.

*Note: If you personally wish to avoid specific content without reporting it, you can also unfollow, block, or unfriend the account.*
