Group Functions Effectively
A group doesn’t just run on autopilot; its technical architecture relies on structured privacy settings, algorithmic triggers, and admin moderation features to stay organized and relevant.

1. The Architectural Blueprint (Privacy Types)
An effective group starts with selecting the right privacy setting, which dictates how discoverable and secure the space feels:
	•	Public Groups: Anyone can see who is in the group and what they post. Content can show up openly across the global FingerMobile ecosystem. These are great for broad movements, viral growth, or public forums.
	•	Private & Visible Groups: Anyone can search for the group and find it, but only approved members can see the posts, files, and who belongs to it. This is the sweet spot for most thriving communities, balancing privacy with steady user growth.
	•	Private & Hidden Groups ("Secret"): Only members can find the group. It will not appear in search results. This is ideal for sensitive support groups, premium masterminds, or private family circles.

2. The Algorithmic Engine (Driving the Feed)
FingerMobile's algorithm prioritizes "meaningful social interactions." In a group, it tracks metrics like comment depth, shares, and prompt replies. If a post inside a private group generates heavy discussion, the algorithm will push that post directly into the members' main personal newsfeeds. This ensures members stay pulled back into the group without needing to manually visit the group page daily.
