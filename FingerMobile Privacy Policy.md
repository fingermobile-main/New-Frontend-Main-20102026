## 1. Introduction

Welcome to FingerMobile ("we," "our," or "us"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our platform, mobile applications, websites, and related services (collectively, the "Services").

We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring transparency about our data practices. This policy applies to all users of FingerMobile, including content creators, consumers, advertisers, and business partners.

By accessing or using our Services, you agree to the terms of this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please discontinue use of our Services.

### Legal Basis for Processing

We process your personal data based on the following legal grounds:
- **Consent:** You have given clear consent for us to process your personal data for a specific purpose
- **Contract:** Processing is necessary for a contract we have with you
- **Legal Obligation:** Processing is necessary to comply with the law
- **Legitimate Interests:** Processing is necessary for our legitimate interests, provided these are not overridden by your rights

## 8. Data Retention

### 8.1 Retention Periods

We retain your data for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy:

**Account Data:**
- Active accounts: Retained until account deletion
- Deleted accounts: Retained for 30 days for recovery, then anonymized
- Suspended accounts: Retained for investigation period plus 2 years

**Content:**
- Published content: Retained while account is active or content is publicly available
- Deleted content: Removed from public view immediately, deleted from backups within 90 days
- Draft content: Retained for 1 year after last modification, then deleted

**Transaction Data:**
- Financial records: Retained for 7 years for tax and audit purposes
- Payment information: Tokenized and retained for recurring billing
- Receipts and invoices: Retained for 7 years

**Communications:**
- Customer support tickets: Retained for 3 years
- In-app messages: Retained for 1 year after conversation ends
- Email communications: Retained for 3 years

**Technical Data:**
- Logs and diagnostics: Retained for 90 days
- Analytics data: Aggregated and retained indefinitely; individual data retained for 26 months
- Security logs: Retained for 1 year

**AI Training Data:**
- Content used for model training: Retained according to model lifecycle
- Prompts and inputs: Anonymized after 30 days unless opted out
- Model versions: Retained for 2 years after replacement

### 8.2 Deletion Requests

You may request deletion of your data:
- Submit request through Privacy Settings or contact privacy@fingermobile.com
- We will respond within 30 days
- Some data may be retained for legal compliance or legitimate business purposes
- Anonymized or aggregated data may be retained

### 8.3 Automatic Deletion

We automatically delete or anonymize data:
- Inactive accounts after 2 years (with prior notification)
- Expired verification documents after 90 days
- Temporary files and caches according to system policies
- Backup data according to retention schedules

## Appendix C: Glossary

- **Personal Data:** Any information relating to an identified or identifiable individual
- **Processing:** Any operation performed on personal data (collection, storage, use, etc.)
- **Controller:** Entity that determines purposes and means of processing
- **Processor:** Entity that processes data on behalf of controller
- **Anonymization:** Irreversibly altering data so individual cannot be identified
- **Pseudonymization:** Processing that replaces identifiers with pseudonyms
