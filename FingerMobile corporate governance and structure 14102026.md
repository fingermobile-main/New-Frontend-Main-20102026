3. Cross-Functional RACI Governance Matrix

⚬ R (Responsible): The role that executes the task to completion.
⚬ A (Accountable): The individual with final veto/approval power; exactly one role per activity.
⚬ C (Consulted): Key stakeholders who provide inputs, technical data, or regulatory validation.
⚬ I (Informed): Stakeholders notified of outcomes, milestones, or finalized decisions.

4. Conflict Resolution & Segregation of Duties (SoD) Safeguards

To prevent operational deadlocks and ensure compliance with global corporate governance codes:

1. Internal Audit Independence Rule:
   Role 5 (Group Chief, Audit & Internal Affairs) holds zero operational approval limits within the DoA. Role 5 can never act as "Responsible" or "Accountable" for spending, operations, or policy design to maintain audit objectivity.
2. Dual-Key Digital Asset Authorization:
   Any transfer of assets from the Infinity Coin reserve treasury exceeding $100,000 requires asymmetric multi-signature execution: Key A held by Role 18 (ED, Blockchain), Key B held by Role 13 (ED, International Treasury), and Key C held by Role 3 (Group CFO), requiring at least 2 of 3 authorizations.
3. Single-Source Procurement Escalation:
   Any purchase order originating from Role 17 (ED, Procurement) that circumvents competitive bidding must be audited for compliance by Role 6 (Policy Compliance) and approved by Role 3 (Group CFO) before contractual commitments can be made.
4. Legal & Compliance Deadlock Protocol:
   If Role 2 (Legal) and Role 6 (Compliance) provide conflicting determinations on cross-border operations or regulatory exposure, the matter is referred directly to the Board Audit & Compliance Committee alongside an independent external legal opinion commissioned by Role 1 (Group Company Secretary).
