About FIC Staking

Staking your FIC tokens in the Vault allows you to earn passive income while contributing to the security and stability of the FingerMobile Protocol ecosystem.

How Staking Works:

  • Lock up your FIC tokens for a specified period to earn rewards
  • Rewards are distributed automatically based on your stake amount and lock-up period
  • Longer lock-up periods offer higher Annual Percentage Yields (APY)
  • You can unstake your tokens at any time, but early withdrawals may incur penalties

Benefits of Staking:

  • Earn passive income with APY rates up to 12.5%
  • Support the network and contribute to ecosystem growth
  • Participate in governance decisions (coming soon)
  • No minimum staking amount required
