# FingerMobile Protocol Copyright & DMCA Policy

**Version:** 2.0.0
**Effective Date:** October 9, 2026
**Governing Entity:** GraceLink Business Technologies Limited
**Governing Law:** Republic of Singapore & International Intellectual Property Accords

---

## 1. Zero Tolerance on Copyright Infringement

GraceLink Business Technologies Limited respects intellectual property rights and expects creators and members to do the same. We expeditiously respond to compliant notices of alleged copyright infringement in accordance with international digital copyright frameworks.

---

## 2. Submitting a DMCA Takedown Notice

To file a valid infringement notice, the copyright owner or authorized representative must submit the following via our **Legal Hub DMCA Console** (`/legal`):

1. **Physical or Electronic Signature** of the authorized agent.
2. **Identification of Copyrighted Work:** Clear description of the protected work claimed to be infringed.
3. **URL of Infringing Material:** Exact link or location of the infringing content on FingerMobile.
4. **Contact Information:** Full name, physical address, telephone number, and valid email address.
5. **Good-Faith Statement:** A sworn statement confirming the claimant's good-faith belief that use of the material is unauthorized.
6. **Accuracy Statement:** A declaration under penalty of perjury that the information provided is accurate.

---

## 3. Counter-Notification Procedure

If your content was removed due to a takedown notice and you believe this was an error or misidentification, you may submit a formal counter-notification containing:

- Your contact details and signature.
- Identification of the removed material and previous URL location.
- A statement consenting to Singapore jurisdiction for dispute adjudication.

Upon receipt of a valid counter-notice, the material may be restored within 10 to 14 business days unless legal action is initiated by the original claimant.
