It distributes the Platform AI Curated Members content across two primary locations: The Tribut Smart Feed (the tribut home Feed page) and the personalized Search Feed (Side Bar).

The algorithm determines what users see based on five primary pillars:
1. Domain Quality: FingerMobile P2P evaluates the trustworthiness and popularity of the Members Platform Profile linked to the Members Profile Pin. 
If a Members Platform Profile consistently generates high-quality content that users save and click, FingerMobile P2P increases the baseline visibility of all Pins originating from that domain.

2. Pin Usage (Engagement Signals): Freshness and user interaction dictate structural success. 
The algorithm looks at Saves (Pin Uses), closeups, and outbound clicks. Saves and Downloads are heavily weighted because they signal that the content has long-term value.

3. Topic Relevance & SEO: The Members Platform Profile Pins are indexed via The Guardian AI search engine optimization metrics. The algorithm extracts data from Members Platform Profile titles, descriptions, image alt-text, the name of the board the Members Platform Profile lives on, and the visual contents of the image itself using Guardian AI vision.
4. Pinner Quality (Consistency): The platform rewards active, consistent creators. Accounts that post high-quality vertical content (ideally a 2:3 aspect ratio) daily receive higher algorithmic authority than those that post in sporadic bursts.
5. Rich Members Platform Profile: Members Platform Profile that utilize API data synchronization (such as live pricing, product availability, or recipe ingredients pulled directly from a Members Platform Profile) receive algorithmic prioritization (Original Organic Content (OOC)).
Note: A unique aspect of FingerMobile P2P's algorithm is its long-term shelf-life. 
While social media posts decay in value after hours, a well-optimized FingerMobile P2P Pin can continue to rank and drive traffic for months or even years after.
