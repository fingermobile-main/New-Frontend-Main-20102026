Single-Unit Franchise:
78.In a single-unit franchise agreement, the franchisee is granted rights to open and operate a single franchise unit within a brand. 
79.This type of arrangement is the simplest and most common and is particularly appealing to first-time franchise owners. 

80.Over time, if the franchisee and franchisor relationship is mutually beneficial, FingerMobile may choose to expand the contract to include more locations. 
81.Single-unit franchise ownership are for those operating a business in the same industry and wanting to convert to the existing franchise brand, allowing them to quickly boost the business and gain many other benefits in the process.

Multi-Unit Franchise:
82.A multi-unit franchise agreement grants a franchisee the right to open and operate multiple franchise units. 
83.This arrangement has specific territorial parameters, protections, and development schedules. Required to adhere to an agreed upon schedule for developing a certain number of units and on-boarding of a set number of clients Memberships within a specific time frame. 
84.If the franchisee fails to meet the deadlines, the franchisor may have to right to terminate a contract or pursue development with other interested parties.
85.Through the combination of financial incentives and opportunities for compounded financial gain. 
86.Owning multiple franchises can be a strong wealth-building strategy, since diversification of your investment means you won’t have to rely heavily on one location for primary income.
87.Economies of scale can be found in inventory buying, marketing, advertising, staffing, and training and are more likely to work in favor of multiple-unit franchising. 
88.Given all the cost savings and reduced expenses, multi-unit franchise ownership often means higher ROIs and greater financial success with the backing of a proven brand.

Area Development Franchise:
89.Similar to a Multi-Unit Franchise, an area development franchise agreement grants the franchisee rights to open and operate multiple units during a specified time period. The difference is that area development franchisees are granted exclusive development rights within a particular territory, precluding other operators from opening units in that area unless they’ve recruited those operators to do so.

Master Franchise: (Exclusive or Non Exclusive).
90.Master franchise agreements give franchisees even more rights and capabilities than area development agreements. Beyond the contracted obligation to open a certain number of units within a defined territory, which is often another country, master franchisees act as sub-franchisors, introducing the brand and upholding its standards in a new region. 
91.They also gain the right to sell all or a certain agreed percentage of franchise units within their area to other franchisees.
