In the BeatwaveNG sovereign audio ecosystem, "SoundRiver" and "SoundOcean" represent a two-tiered architectural and economic model designed to democratize youth creator publishing while giving audiophiles and professional artists sovereign economic independence:

1. Conceptual Metaphors
Tier	Metaphor	Purpose
🌊 SoundRiver	A free-flowing river	Open, fast, and accessible to everyone. No barriers, paywalls, or gatekeepers. Designed for emergent youth artists, indie podcasters, and open-source public audio exploration.
💎 SoundOcean	The deep, boundless ocean	Pure depth, master fidelity, and economic sovereignty. Built for studio-grade audiophile listening, multi-track stem downloads, and direct creator monetization.

4. Why This Architecture Exists
Eliminating the Gatekeepers: Traditional platforms charge indie creators distribution fees or require middleman labels before their music can be discovered. SoundRiver ensures any youth creator with a smartphone or laptop can upload and stream immediately with zero financial friction.

Sovereign Creator Revenue: When artists are ready to monetize studio-grade work, SoundOcean bypasses centralized label streaming payouts (fractions of a cent) in favor of peer-to-peer FIC smart-contract tipping and direct listener ownership of sovereign audio stems.
